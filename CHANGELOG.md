## [Unreleased]

### Changed

- **Breaking:** sent letters are final. Their words, title, banner, stickers, locks and notification text can no longer be changed. To fix one, unsend it (only possible until it's opened), edit it, and send it again.
- A sent letter on the writing page shows a preview of exactly what the reader sees, with its replies underneath.
- Opened letters can no longer be deleted.
- Notifications now go to whoever is signed in on a phone, and stop when you log out. Before, a phone kept getting the last account's notifications.
